# Guppy Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: April 23, 2026**

Guppy is an offline, local music player. Privacy is a core part of how Guppy is designed: all of your music, listening history, and personal data stay on your device. We do not operate any servers, and we do not collect, upload, or share your data in any way.

This policy explains what Guppy accesses on your device, why it accesses it, and the control you have.

## Information We Do Not Collect

Guppy **does not** collect, upload, or share any of the following with us or any third party:

- Account information, name, email, phone number, or any personal identifier.
- Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or location data.
- Your music files, lyrics, artwork, listening history, favorites, or playlists.
- Usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.

Guppy does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## What Guppy Accesses On Your Device

Guppy only accesses the following items on your device when you actively use the related feature. Every access is gated by Apple's system permission prompts, and everything happens locally on your device.

### Local Music and Files

- Guppy reads music or lyrics files only when you explicitly pick them through the system file picker.
- Guppy can **only access the files you have explicitly selected**. It does not scan or read any other files on your device.

### Photos

- Guppy accesses your photo library only when you choose to import artwork or lyric images from it.
- Guppy uses the system photo picker (PHPicker), which gives Guppy access **only to the specific images you select**. Guppy never requests full photo library access.

### Local Network

Guppy uses your local network only for the following features that you explicitly enable. All connections are limited to your local network or to the destinations you configure yourself; nothing is uploaded to us or any third party:

- **Device-to-device transfer**: move music and lyrics between two devices running Guppy on the same local network.
- **Browser upload to phone**: temporarily start a local HTTP service on your phone so you can upload files from a browser on the same network. This service runs only while you have the feature open.
- **WebDAV**: connect to a WebDAV server that you configure yourself in order to browse or import music.
- **Subsonic-compatible servers**: connect to a Subsonic server that you configure yourself in order to browse or import music.

Any server addresses, usernames, and passwords you enter are stored only on your device (credentials are kept in the system Keychain) and are never uploaded.

## Third-Party Services

When you choose to connect to a WebDAV or Subsonic server, the resulting data exchange happens between your device and the server you configured, and is governed by that service's own privacy policy. Guppy is not involved in any data collection or forwarding beyond that direct interaction.

## Your Responsibilities and Music Copyright

- Guppy **only plays** local music that you already legally own or are authorized to use.
- Guppy **does not provide any music download, search, or distribution service**. All music, lyrics, and artwork are imported by you.
- You are solely responsible for ensuring that you hold the necessary rights or licenses for any music, lyrics, or artwork you import. You bear all responsibility for any consequences arising from importing or using unauthorized content.

## Children's Privacy

Guppy does not collect personal information from any user, including children.
